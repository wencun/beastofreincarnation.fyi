# Editorial Policy

Beast of Reincarnation Guide is an independent fan resource. Our purpose is to help players make a specific decision or solve a specific problem—not to repeat news, pad pages, or place advertising around copied information.

## How we label information

- **Officially confirmed** means the statement is supported by a publisher, developer, platform-holder, or official support source linked on the page.
- **Tested in-game** means the result was reproduced in the current retail game. A page must state the platform and version checked.
- **Community-reported** means players have reported the issue or outcome, but we have not established it as a universal result. These reports are not presented as a fix.
- **Unconfirmed** means there is not enough evidence to make the claim. We do not turn an unconfirmed rumor into a guide page.

## Sources and originality

We write our own explanations, organize evidence around the player’s question, and link to material sources where useful. We do not copy third-party walkthroughs, database entries, screenshots, or video transcripts. We do not publish a weapon location, quest step, ranking, or performance claim merely because it is a popular search term.

## Updates and corrections

Game patches can change settings, progression, and performance. We review time-sensitive pages when an official update changes their subject and identify the version or date checked. If you find an error, use [Report an error](/en/report-error) with the affected URL, platform, game version, and a source or reproduction detail. Corrections take priority over preserving an older conclusion.

## Independence and disclosure

This site is not affiliated with GAME FREAK, Fictions, or platform holders. We do not accept payment in exchange for a favorable conclusion. If that changes, the relevant page will identify the relationship clearly.
