# What Can Emma and Koo Do?

<div class="meta"><span>Last updated: Aug 4, 2026</span><span>Version: Launch information</span><span>PC · PS5</span><span class="verified">Officially confirmed</span></div>

<div class="quick-answer"><strong>Quick Answer</strong>Emma fights with a sword and uses her plant-like hair for traversal and falling assassinations. Koo supports her through command-selected skills. Together, they form one combat unit: Emma handles immediate movement and action, while Koo provides tactical techniques selected from a slowed command interface.</div>

## Prerequisites
- No progression requirement is confirmed for the basic abilities described here.

## Steps
1. Use Emma’s sword actions for direct combat.
2. Look for terrain where Emma’s vine-like hair enables traversal.
3. Use height and positioning for the demonstrated falling assassination.
4. Open Koo’s command menu to select a supporting technique.
5. Adapt your loadout through the published skill tree, gear, and spirit-stone systems.
