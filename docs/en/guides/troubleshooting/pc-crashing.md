# Beast of Reincarnation PC Crashing

<div class="meta"><span>Last checked: Aug 26, 2026</span><span>Steam · PC Game Pass</span><span class="verified">Official support guidance</span></div>

<div class="quick-answer"><strong>Quick Answer</strong>Fictions asks affected PC players to submit a support ticket so the issue can be investigated. Include your CrashDump when available. The official article says this is usually located at <code>C:\Users\YourUsername\AppData\Local\CrashDumps</code>.</div>

## Do this first

1. Install the current game update. The latest official patch is **v1.0.9 / v1.007.000**.
2. Record whether the crash happens on launch, in a specific area, after changing a setting, or after a fixed amount of playtime.
3. Record your platform (Steam or PC Game Pass), game version, Windows version, GPU, and driver version.
4. Keep the newest relevant crash dump if Windows created one.
5. Submit a ticket through [Fictions Support](https://support.fictions.com/hc/en-us/categories/42992376012691-Beast-of-Reincarnation).
6. If you update BIOS, use only your device or motherboard maker's official instructions. A BIOS update can help some crashes, but it has risk; do not update it solely on the basis of an unverified community workaround.

## What to include in the ticket

- Exact time and frequency of crashes
- Steps that reproduce the crash
- CrashDump, if available
- Game version and platform
- A screenshot or short video of the error, with private information removed

## What is officially confirmed

Fictions has an active support article for PC crashes on Steam and PC Game Pass. It directs players to submit a ticket, include CrashDump files if possible, and notes that a BIOS update can sometimes resolve crashes.

Patch v1.0.9 lists several PC fixes, but it does **not** claim that every PC crash has been fixed. Do not describe an individual workaround as a confirmed universal fix.
