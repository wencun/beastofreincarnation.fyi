# Beast of Reincarnation Dark Interiors

<div class="meta"><span>Last checked: Aug 10, 2026</span><span>Display issue status</span><span class="verified">No official fix confirmed</span></div>

<div class="quick-answer"><strong>Quick Answer</strong>We have not found an official Fictions support article that confirms a fix for overly dark interior scenes. Do not install unverified configuration files or claim that a specific setting solves every case. First identify whether the issue affects gameplay, cutscenes, or only a particular display.</div>

## Safe checks to report

1. Note the platform, display type, HDR setting, game version, and affected area.
2. Capture a screenshot or short video that shows the issue without account details.
3. Compare one affected interior with one unaffected outdoor area.
4. If you change a display or in-game setting, record the before/after result rather than assuming it is a universal fix.
5. Send the information through [Fictions Support](https://support.fictions.com/hc/en-us/categories/42992376012691-Beast-of-Reincarnation).

## Current official status

v1.0.7 includes camera, text-size, cutscene, settings, balance, and bug fixes. Its published notes do not list a dark-interior fix. Future patches are planned, but no date or specific lighting fix has been announced.
