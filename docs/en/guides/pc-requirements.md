# Beast of Reincarnation PC Requirements

<div class="meta"><span>Last checked: Aug 16, 2026</span><span>Source: official Steam listing</span><span>Windows PC</span><span class="verified">Official requirements</span></div>

<div class="quick-answer"><strong>Quick Answer</strong>Beast of Reincarnation requires a 64-bit PC, <strong>16 GB RAM</strong>, <strong>45 GB</strong> of available SSD space, and DirectX 12. The official minimum target is Low 1080p at 40 FPS with DLSS or FSR; the recommended target is Medium 1080p at 60 FPS with DLSS or FSR3.</div>

## Official system requirements

| | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OS | Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) | Windows 11 (64-bit) |
| CPU | Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 3600 | Intel Core i7-12700K or AMD Ryzen 7 5800X |
| Memory | 16 GB RAM | 16 GB RAM |
| GPU | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8 GB) or AMD Radeon RX 580 (8 GB) | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12 GB) or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT (12 GB) |
| DirectX | Version 12 | Version 12 |
| Storage | 45 GB available space; SSD required | 45 GB available space; SSD required |
| Official performance note | Low, 1080p at 40 FPS with DLSS (RTX 20–50 Series) or FSR1 (Performance) | Medium, 1080p at 60 FPS with DLSS or FSR3 (Performance) |

## Check your PC

1. Open **Settings → System → About** and confirm you use 64-bit Windows.
2. Check your CPU and installed RAM in **About** or **Task Manager**.
3. In Task Manager, open **Performance** to confirm the GPU model and available video memory.
4. Keep at least 45 GB free on an SSD before installing.
5. Compare your hardware with the table above; meeting the minimum target is not the same as meeting the recommended 60 FPS target.
