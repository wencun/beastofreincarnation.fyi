# Beast of Reincarnation Best Weapons: Choose the Right Sword

<div class="meta"><span>Last checked: Sep 8, 2026</span><span>Build planning</span><span class="verified">No invented weapon rankings</span></div>

<div class="quick-answer"><strong>Quick Answer</strong>There is no single best weapon for every Beast of Reincarnation build. The useful choice is the sword whose effect supports the enemy you are facing and the way you build Emma and Koo. Upgrade one dependable main option before spreading scarce resources across several untested weapons.</div>

## Pick a weapon by the problem you need to solve

| Your current problem | Prioritize | Avoid assuming |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You are still learning enemies | A dependable weapon you can keep upgraded | That a late-game list is automatically stronger at your current upgrade level |
| A boss resists your usual approach | A weapon effect that creates a different opening or status plan | That raw attack alone decides every matchup |
| You rely on parries and Koo commands | A setup that leaves you comfortable executing the core combat loop | That a community “best build” fits your current skills and Spirit Stones |

## A reliable upgrade order

1. Choose one main sword that feels consistent in regular encounters.
2. Upgrade it before investing broadly in side options.
3. Keep a second option only when it solves a documented matchup problem.
4. Test one change at a time in a familiar encounter, then keep notes on what changed.

## Why this is better than a generic tier list

Weapon locations, patch balance, upgrade level, and player comfort can change the answer. A ranking that does not name the game version, the weapon level, and the reason for each recommendation is not enough evidence to spend your materials. This guide will add specific locations only after they are checked against the current retail build.
