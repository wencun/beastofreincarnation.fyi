# Beast of Reincarnation Difficulty Settings: Story, Normal, and Hard

<div class="meta"><span>Last checked: Sep 8, 2026</span><span>Gameplay settings</span><span class="verified">Official support and developer statements</span></div>

<div class="quick-answer"><strong>Quick Answer</strong>Beast of Reincarnation has three difficulty settings: <strong>Story</strong>, <strong>Normal</strong>, and <strong>Hard</strong>. If parry timing or incoming damage is stopping your progress, start with Story; it is the appropriate choice for learning the combat loop, not a workaround or an incomplete version of the game.</div>

## Which difficulty should you choose?

| Choose | Good fit when | What to remember |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story | You want room to learn enemy tells, parries, and Koo’s command loop | The developer describes the game as playable for people who are not comfortable with fast action. |
| Normal | You already enjoy action RPG timing and want the standard first playthrough | Fictions’ v1.0.7 notes include normal-enemy balance changes for Normal. |
| Hard | You want a more demanding combat challenge and accept repeated attempts | Do not assume undocumented damage, drop-rate, or achievement rules. Check the current game version first. |

## What is confirmed

- A developer reply on the official Steam discussion board confirms three difficulty settings.
- Fictions’ v1.0.7 notes refer to boss adjustments across all difficulty levels and normal-enemy adjustments on Normal.
- Difficulty does not replace the core loop: read attacks, use Emma’s defense, then spend the resource you build on Koo’s commands.

## What not to assume

Official patch notes do not publish every numerical modifier or every rule around changing a setting mid-playthrough. Avoid old guides that state exact multipliers or completion requirements without naming their game version and evidence.

## A practical first-hour setup

1. Install the current patch before judging a boss, camera behavior, or subtitle option.
2. Choose the setting that lets you recognize attacks rather than repeatedly absorb them.
3. Change one option at a time, then play a few encounters to see what actually helps.
4. If visibility or text is the issue, check camera, subtitle, controller, and cutscene settings as well—not only difficulty.
