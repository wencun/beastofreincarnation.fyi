# Missing Pre-order Bonus or Deluxe DLC?

<div class="meta"><span>Last updated: Aug 7, 2026</span><span>Purchase support</span><span>Steam · PS5 · Xbox</span><span class="verified">Official contents checked</span></div>

<div class="quick-answer"><strong>Quick Answer</strong>A missing bonus is usually an entitlement or purchase-channel issue, not a gameplay setting. Confirm the edition, purchase channel, and owned add-on first. Then contact the store that processed the order with the evidence below. This page does not promise a universal fix.</div>

## What should be included?

The official Steam announcement lists the Brown Shiba Skin for Koo and 30,000 Amber as the pre-order bonus. Deluxe content is separate and can vary from the time-limited pre-order reward. Check the live store listing for your region before assuming that an item was included.

- [Official Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2001760)
- [Official Xbox edition details](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/beast-of-reincarnation-pre-order-bundle/9NGQ6JMZ0X2Q)

## Before contacting support

1. Confirm the purchase date, store, platform, and edition.
2. Keep the receipt or order confirmation.
3. Confirm that you launched the game with the same platform account that owns it.
4. Check the game's DLC or installed-content entry in the platform client.
5. Fully restart the platform client, then launch the game once. This can refresh the client, but is not a guaranteed entitlement fix.
6. Capture purchase, installed-content, in-game, and game-version screenshots.

Do not post an order number, key, email address, or account screenshot in a public discussion.

## Who should you contact first?

| Purchase channel | First contact | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steam direct purchase | Steam Support, then Purchases | Steam processed the order and can check the account purchase. |
| Third-party key seller | The seller's support | The seller must first confirm that the supplied key and listed bonus were correct. |
| PlayStation Store | PlayStation Support | The order and add-on entitlement are tied to the PSN account. |
| Xbox or Microsoft Store | Xbox Support | The order and add-on entitlement are tied to the Microsoft account. |
| Physical edition | Retailer, then follow the included code instructions | Eligibility can depend on the retailer or a pack-in code. |

If the store confirms that the entitlement exists but the content is still missing in-game, include that support case number when contacting the publisher's official support channel.
