## What should be included?

The official Steam information lists the Brown Shiba Skin for Koo and 30,000 Amber as the pre-order bonus. Deluxe content is separate and eligibility can vary by edition, store, region, and purchase date. Check your receipt and the live store listing before assuming an item was included.

## If content is still missing

1. Confirm the purchase date, store, platform, and edition.
2. Launch the game with the same platform account that owns the purchase.
3. Check the game's DLC or installed-content entry in the platform client.
4. Fully restart the platform client, then launch the game once.
5. Keep the receipt and screenshots of the DLC entry, in-game inventory, and game version.
6. Contact the store that processed the order and ask it to confirm the entitlement on your account.

Do not post an order number, key, email address, or account screenshot in a public discussion.

## Who should you contact first?

| Purchase channel | First contact |
| --- | --- |
| Steam direct purchase | Steam Support → Purchases |
| Third-party key seller | The seller's support |
| PlayStation Store | PlayStation Support |
| Xbox or Microsoft Store | Xbox Support |
| Physical edition | Retailer, then the included-code instructions |

If the store confirms the entitlement exists but the item remains unavailable in-game, include that support case number when contacting the publisher's official support channel.
