## Confirmed cast information

- **Emma — Yui Ishikawa.** The published cast announcement identifies Ishikawa as the protagonist’s Japanese voice actor.
- **Akio Otsuka — featured cast.** Published coverage of the reveal names Otsuka among the actors announced with the game.

Cast lists can be incomplete or differ by language. We will not fill an exhaustive list with unsourced database entries; use the credits and the current official material for a complete in-game record.

## Change voice-over language safely

1. Install the current patch.
2. Open the game’s language or audio settings and choose the displayed voice-over option.
3. Restart once and confirm the preference has remained selected.
4. If the selection resets, note your platform and version before contacting [Fictions Support](https://support.fictions.com/hc/en-us/categories/42992376012691-Beast-of-Reincarnation).

Patch v1.0.10 specifically lists a fix for voice-language settings resetting on restart. Do not modify game files just to change spoken language.
