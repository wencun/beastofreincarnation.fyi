# Is Beast of Reincarnation Open World?

**Not confirmed as a label:** official materials describe a changing blighted forest and an expansive action RPG world, but they do not define the game with a specific “open world” structure.

## The useful answer

Do not rely on a store tag alone. Until the developer publishes a direct description of area structure, treat claims about map size, seamless travel, side-activity density, and world-wide freedom as unconfirmed.
