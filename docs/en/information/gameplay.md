# Beast of Reincarnation Gameplay Explained

**Officially confirmed:** the game combines Emma's real-time sword abilities with commands that direct Koo to use techniques. Xbox describes this as a fusion of real-time combat and command-based strategy.

## What that means

The core relationship is not a solo action system with a cosmetic companion. Emma and Koo are designed to fight as a unit. For published details on their abilities, see [Emma and Koo](/en/guides/emma-koo) and [How combat works](/en/guides/combat-system).
